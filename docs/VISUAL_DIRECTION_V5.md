# Al.Kim.ia — Visual Direction V05

## Formula
Unreal-like atmosphere + TouchDesigner-like generative behavior + contemporary biomorphic sculpture + experimental cinema + architectural negative space.

## Entities
Near-black charcoal, highly matte, tactile, asymmetric, heavy and silent. Identity comes from behavior: Ale leaks cohesion; Kim holds it. Interior light is rare and appears through reaction/contact, not as bright colored bodies.

## Concert
Warm red/amber darkness, huge scale, crowd as living mass, large suspended sphere, RAWA embedded in stage architecture. At encounter the world does not disappear; attention compresses around the pair.

## Room / Portal
The room is mineral and almost empty. Portal is a real spatial event, not a glowing rectangle. The meadow is rendered live through the opening. Light, particles and implied wind leak into the room.

## Meadow
A landscape rather than a plane. Foreground/midground/background/horizon/sky must read. Climate is explicit. Lavender must read as flowers through stems and clustered heads, with density bands and multiple depths.

## Wind
Narrative system. Gusts cross the field spatially and affect plants, particles, haze and later audio. Avoid identical sinusoidal motion.

## TouchDesigner layer
Use generative fields where memory becomes unstable: portal, contact, tunnel, ascension. Avoid constant glitch, neon RGB splitting or music-visualizer aesthetics.

## Ascension
Do not translate whole plants upward. Flowers lose gravity as fragments. A wave of violet particles travels through the field, entities partially merge, then camera language expands from intimate to global to sky.
