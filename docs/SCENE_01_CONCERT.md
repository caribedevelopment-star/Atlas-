# Memory 01 — The Encounter

## Narrative

The WiZink concert is remembered rather than reconstructed literally.

The arena is dark, circular and immense. A huge warm sphere hangs above the crowd. Hundreds of shadow-like presences form a dense sea of bodies without becoming realistic humans. Ale is the only controlled entity. Kim exists inside the crowd as a warm narrative presence.

The player moves toward Kim. The closer Ale gets, the more the world changes: crowd/noise recede, particles increase, both bodies react, and a filament of matter begins to connect them. Contact ends the memory and leads toward the next threshold: The Room.

## Visual language

### Sphere
- Extremely bright amber/orange source
- Main visual anchor
- Large atmospheric halo
- Strong enough to tint the crowd and fog

### Crowd
- Dense
- Abstract/non-human
- Repetitive enough to read as multitude, irregular enough not to feel procedural
- Dark silhouettes with minimal warm highlights

### Ale + Kim
- Sculptural, malleable matter
- Organic tubes / masses rather than anatomy
- No clear head/torso/arms/legs hierarchy
- Slight inner light only when the material reacts
- Ale cooler, Kim slightly warmer, but both primarily dark matter

### Camera
1. Start global, showing sphere + crowd + arena scale.
2. Descend into third-person follow.
3. During the encounter, camera partially takes control and frames both entities.

## Interaction

Desktop: WASD / arrows.
Mobile: minimal directional touch controls.

No "next scene" button. The scene progresses by proximity and contact.
