# Visual Direction — Al.Kim.ia

## Core aesthetic

Adult, abstract, melancholic, cinematic, architectural and intimate.

## Entity rule

If the silhouette clearly reads as a conventional human body with a standard head/torso/arms/legs hierarchy, it is too humanoid.

Entities should feel like:
- malleable matter
- sculptural tubes / folded organic masses
- dark mineral or rubber-like surfaces
- subtle internal light
- slow breathing deformation
- particles only when emotionally activated

## Concert rule

The WiZink memory should be translated rather than copied literally.

Keep:
- circular arena scale
- dense crowd
- orange/red concert atmosphere
- huge suspended glowing sphere
- sense of being lost inside a mass of people

Abstract:
- spectators into shadow presences
- stage into luminous geometry
- arena architecture into rings and darkness
- encounter into material reaction rather than literal human gesture

## Typography

The name **Al.Kim.ia** should feel bold, compact, editorial and intentional. Use very heavy weight, tight tracking for large display type, and wide tracking for small labels.
